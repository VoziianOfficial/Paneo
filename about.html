About Paneo

Built for homeowners who want clearer window provider options.

Paneo is an independent matching platform for window projects. The goal is simple: help homeowners start with organized categories, clear expectations, and relevant local provider options.

Explore Services Request Quotes

Paneo is not a direct window contractor or installer.

Our story

A quieter alternative to messy contractor searches.

Paneo was created around a common homeowner problem: window projects often start with too many tabs, unclear service descriptions, and providers that may or may not fit the project.

Instead of acting like a contractor, Paneo focuses on the first step: helping users describe the project and compare relevant independent window companies.

Focused on comparison, not direct service.

The platform does not install, repair, replace, price, inspect, or warranty windows. Those conversations happen directly between the homeowner and independent providers.
