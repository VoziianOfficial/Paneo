Cookie Policy

This page explains how Paneo may use cookies and similar technologies to support site functionality and privacy preferences.

Simple preference control

Visitors can accept or decline the cookie notice. The choice is stored locally so the banner does not repeat unnecessarily.

Privacy Policy Cookie Policy Terms of Service

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies may also be used for basic site functionality and preference storage.

2. How Paneo May Use Cookies

Paneo may use cookies to remember privacy choices, support form behavior, improve browsing experience, understand general site activity, and maintain basic website functionality.

3. Cookie Preference Banner

When you first visit this website, a privacy preference banner may appear. You may accept or decline. The banner remains visible until a choice is made.

4. Local Storage

This website may use browser local storage to remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie notice. This helps avoid repeatedly showing the same banner on future visits.

5. Third-Party Tools

Some embedded features, such as maps, fonts, icons, or analytics tools, may be provided by third parties. Those services may use their own cookies or tracking technologies according to their own policies.

6. Managing Cookies

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how certain parts of the website function.

7. Contact

For cookie-related questions, contact us at hello@paneomatch.com.
