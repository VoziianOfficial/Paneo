Compare smarter

Questions worth asking before accepting a custom window estimate.

Custom projects can include more variables than standard window work. These questions help make provider responses easier to compare.

What materials and glass options are included?

Ask whether the estimate includes frame material, glass type, hardware, finish color, trim, grille style, and any required accessories.

Is measurement included before final pricing?

Custom designs often require exact measurements before final pricing can be confirmed. Ask when measurements happen and whether they affect the final estimate.

Can the provider show similar project experience?

For non-standard shapes or premium design requests, ask about previous custom work, design process, ordering timeline, and installation limitations.

What happens if the scope changes?

Confirm how extra work, hidden damage, design adjustments, delayed materials, or structural changes would be priced and approved.

FAQ

Questions before comparing custom window providers.

Is Paneo a custom window design company?

No. Paneo is an independent matching platform. We do not design, manufacture, install, repair, inspect, or replace windows directly.

What should I include in a custom window request?

Helpful details include your preferred style, window size or shape, frame color, material preferences, glass goals, project timing, ZIP code, and any photos or notes about the existing opening.

Can custom windows cost more than standard windows?

Often, yes. Custom size, shape, material, glass performance, finish choices, measurements, lead time, and installation complexity can all affect pricing.

What should I verify before hiring a provider?

Confirm license, insurance, written estimate, measurement process, ordering timeline, warranty terms, payment schedule, and final project scope directly with the provider.
