FAQ

Clear answers before you compare.

How do I compare local window providers?

Start by choosing a service category, then request provider options based on your project type, location, timing, and window preferences.

Is Paneo a window installation company?

No. Paneo is an independent matching platform. Contractors and providers are independent, and homeowners choose who they contact or hire.

Are quotes from providers usually free?

Many providers offer free estimates, but quote policies can vary. Homeowners should confirm pricing and terms directly with each provider.

What affects window project pricing?

Pricing may depend on window size, glass type, frame material, quantity, labor, property access, location, and the provider selected.
