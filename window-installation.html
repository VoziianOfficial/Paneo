Compare smarter

Questions worth asking before accepting an installation estimate.

Installation projects can include product, labor, prep, and finishing details. These questions help make provider responses easier to compare.

What exactly is included in the installation quote?

Ask whether the quote includes windows, labor, measurements, removal, flashing, sealing, trim, cleanup, disposal, and any finishing work.

When are final measurements taken?

Confirm whether the provider takes exact measurements before ordering and whether measurement results can change final pricing.

How will the provider handle sealing and weather protection?

Ask about flashing, insulation, air sealing, water management, frame preparation, and how installation quality is checked.

What could change the final price or timeline?

Confirm how hidden damage, difficult access, product delays, structural issues, extra trim, or scope changes would be priced and approved.

FAQ

Questions before comparing window installation providers.

Is Paneo a window installation company?

No. Paneo is an independent matching platform. We do not install, replace, repair, inspect, manufacture, or recommend windows directly.

What should I include in a window installation request?

Helpful details include window count, approximate sizes, home type, whether openings already exist, preferred frame or glass type, ZIP code, timing, and any access concerns.

Can installation pricing change after measurements?

It can. Final pricing may depend on exact measurements, product selection, existing condition, access, trim work, hidden damage, and provider scope.

What should I verify before hiring an installer?

Confirm license, insurance, written estimate, measurement process, installation scope, warranty terms, payment schedule, cleanup, and final pricing directly with the provider.
