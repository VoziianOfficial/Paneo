Compare smarter

Questions worth asking before accepting an energy window estimate.

Energy-efficient windows can involve technical product details. These questions help homeowners compare provider explanations more clearly.

What glass package is included in the estimate?

Ask whether the quote includes double-pane, triple-pane, Low-E coating, gas fill, spacer type, privacy glass, or other performance upgrades.

How will the provider address drafts or air leakage?

Ask whether the provider evaluates existing frames, sealing, weatherstripping, installation method, and surrounding trim or wall conditions.

Are product ratings and warranties clearly documented?

Request written details about ratings, manufacturer warranty, glass seal coverage, labor warranty, and any limits or exclusions.

What factors could change the final price?

Confirm how frame damage, hidden moisture, difficult access, custom sizes, extra trim, or scope changes would be priced and approved.

FAQ

Questions before comparing energy-efficient window providers.

Is Paneo an energy-efficient window installer?

No. Paneo is an independent matching platform. We do not install, replace, repair, inspect, manufacture, or recommend windows directly.

What details should I include in an energy-efficient window request?

Helpful details include window age, room comfort issues, drafts, condensation, noise concerns, number of windows, glass preferences, ZIP code, and project timing.

Are energy-efficient windows always more expensive?

Costs vary. Glass package, frame material, size, quantity, labor, installation complexity, and selected provider can all affect the estimate.

What should I verify before choosing a provider?

Confirm license, insurance, product ratings, written estimate, installation scope, glass package, warranty terms, payment schedule, and final pricing directly.
