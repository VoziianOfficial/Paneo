Compare smarter

Questions worth asking before accepting window consultation advice.

Consultation should make the next step clearer. These questions help homeowners compare provider explanations without rushing into the wrong service.

Will the provider explain repair, replacement, and upgrade options?

Ask whether the consultation compares multiple paths or only recommends one solution. A clear explanation can help you understand tradeoffs.

Is the consultation free, paid, or credited later?

Confirm whether there is a consultation fee, travel fee, inspection fee, or credit toward future work before scheduling.

Will I receive written recommendations?

Ask whether the provider gives written notes, photos, measurements, product suggestions, or a detailed estimate after the consultation.

What details are needed before final pricing?

Confirm whether final pricing depends on measurements, product selection, hidden damage, access, custom ordering, or scope confirmation.

FAQ

Questions before comparing window consultation providers.

Is Paneo a window consultation company?

No. Paneo is an independent matching platform. We do not inspect, consult, install, repair, replace, manufacture, or recommend windows directly.

When should I request a window consultation?

Consultation can make sense when you are unsure whether you need repair, replacement, custom design, energy upgrades, or a more detailed inspection from a provider.

What should I include in a consultation request?

Helpful details include the window issue, photos if available, home type, number of windows, comfort concerns, design goals, budget range, ZIP code, and timing.

What should I verify before following provider advice?

Confirm license, insurance, written notes, estimate terms, inspection scope, pricing, warranties, and whether any recommendation is tied to a specific product or service.
