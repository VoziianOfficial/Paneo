Quote factors

01

Window count and size

Larger windows, multiple units, custom sizes, specialty shapes, and exact measurements affect pricing.

02

Glass package

Double-pane, triple-pane, Low-E coatings, gas fills, privacy glass, and safety glass can vary in cost.

03

Frame material

Vinyl, wood, fiberglass, aluminum, composite, and clad frames may differ in price and performance.

04

Existing condition

Rot, water damage, old trim, wall condition, access, disposal, or hidden issues may change the scope.

Compare smarter

Questions worth asking before accepting a replacement estimate.

Replacement projects can include product, labor, removal, finishing, and warranty details. These questions help make provider responses easier to compare.

Is this insert replacement or full-frame replacement?

Ask the provider to explain which method is recommended and why. The answer may depend on frame condition, trim, damage, efficiency goals, and budget.

What product and glass package are included?

Confirm frame material, glass type, Low-E coating, pane count, gas fill, hardware, finish color, grids, and any energy-performance details.

What exactly is included in the quote?

Ask whether the estimate includes measurements, removal, disposal, installation, sealing, flashing, trim, cleanup, and final walkthrough.

What could change the final price?

Confirm how hidden damage, rot, difficult access, product upgrades, custom sizing, extra trim, or scope changes would be priced and approved.

FAQ

Questions before comparing window replacement providers.

Is Paneo a window replacement company?

No. Paneo is an independent matching platform. We do not replace, install, repair, inspect, manufacture, or recommend windows directly.

What should I include in a window replacement request?

Helpful details include window count, approximate sizes, window age, visible damage, draft or comfort issues, preferred style, ZIP code, timing, and photos if available.

Should I repair or replace my windows?

That depends on the issue. Minor hardware or sealing problems may be repairable, while severe rot, failed seals, old inefficient units, or repeated problems may lead providers to recommend replacement.

What should I verify before hiring a replacement provider?

Confirm license, insurance, written estimate, replacement method, product details, glass package, warranty terms, timeline, payment schedule, cleanup, and final pricing directly.
